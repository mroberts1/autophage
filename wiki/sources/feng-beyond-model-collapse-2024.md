## Abstract

> The paper examines how Large Language Models trained on data generated by other LLMs face performance degradation called model collapse. The researchers propose using verification mechanisms on synthesized data to mitigate this issue. They develop theoretical models using Gaussian mixtures and linear classifiers to identify when verifiers can effectively select high-quality generated samples. Experiments on transformer-based matrix eigenvalue computation and LLM-based news summarization demonstrate that "verifiers, even imperfect ones, can indeed be harnessed to prevent model collapse" and that their proposed metrics correlate strongly with performance outcomes.
