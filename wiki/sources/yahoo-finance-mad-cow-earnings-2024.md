## Summary

Finance/business-press framing of the model collapse problem, published April 12, 2024, timed to Q1 earnings season. Situates MAD within growing skepticism about AI investment returns.

Key points:
- Growing wall Street skepticism about AI returns despite massive investment; AI mentions no longer automatically boosted stock sentiment
- The training data scarcity problem: as human-created content is exhausted, companies turn to synthetic data, creating the feedback loop
- Rice University's MAD research cited: "feeding cattle with the remains of other cattle" — the mad cow disease analogy
- Energy demand as a parallel constraint: AI data centers projected to consume 20–25% of U.S. power by end of decade (Arm CEO)
- ChatGPT query uses ~10× more electricity than a Google search (IEA)
- Big tech investing in proprietary chips (Google, Meta) to reduce hardware dependency

The piece contextualizes MAD not as a pure research curiosity but as a concrete risk to AI business models.
