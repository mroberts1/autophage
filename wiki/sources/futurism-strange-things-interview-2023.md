## Summary

Follow-up interview piece by the same Futurism reporter, published August 1, 2023 (about three weeks after the initial coverage). Goes deeper into the implications with the Rice researchers.

Key content:
- "In just five cycles of training on synthetic data" systems can deteriorate significantly — the "five iterations" figure became one of the most widely cited data points
- Researchers describe the problem as "data inbreeding": repeated cycles cause outputs to become monotonous and drift from reality
- Watermarking synthetic content as a mitigation is discussed but noted to introduce its own artifacts that compound over iterations
- Broader concern: search engine quality degradation as AI-generated content floods the web
- The possibility of "loops upon loops" — AI-generated content referencing other AI-generated content in search results

This piece is more expansive than the news brief; includes direct quotes from the researchers and discussion of mitigations.
