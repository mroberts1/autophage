## Summary

Tech-trade coverage of the [[alemohammad-self-consuming-go-mad-2023]] preprint. Key points:

- Training AI on AI-generated content causes progressive quality deterioration (the autophagous loop)
- The self-consuming cycle is already underway: datasets sourced from the internet increasingly contain undisclosed synthetic content
- "Without enough fresh real data in each generation of an autophagous loop, future generative models are doomed to have their quality or diversity progressively decrease"
- As platforms restrict data scraping to protect training material, obtaining fresh real data becomes harder — a compounding problem

Tone is tech-enthusiast/consumer press; accurate summary of the underlying paper.
