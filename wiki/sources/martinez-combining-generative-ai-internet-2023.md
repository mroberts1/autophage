## Abstract

> The paper examines how generative AI systems will perform when trained on datasets containing both original and AI-generated content. The researchers explore whether these systems will "evolve with the new data sets or degenerate" and whether biases will emerge. Using preliminary simulations with an image-generation tool, they report that "the quality of the generated images degrades as more AI-generated data is used for training," suggesting potential degradation effects. The authors acknowledge these findings are preliminary and require further investigation to understand interactions between generative AI and Internet data.
