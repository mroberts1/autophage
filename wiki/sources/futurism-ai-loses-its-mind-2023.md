## Summary

The article covers the early preprint of [[alemohammad-self-consuming-go-mad-2023]] from Rice and Stanford. Key framing:

- Researchers coined "Model Autophagy Disorder" (MAD) for the self-consuming degradation process
- Image models deteriorated visibly after five iterations of training on synthetic data
- The core danger: AI training datasets are scraped from the internet, which increasingly contains AI-generated content
- Datasets like LAION-5B (used to train Stable Diffusion) already contain synthetic images from earlier generations
- The paper was unpublished/pre-peer-review at time of writing

The article notes the practical downstream stakes: search quality degradation, web ecosystems filling with synthetic content "referencing other synthetic material — loops upon loops."
