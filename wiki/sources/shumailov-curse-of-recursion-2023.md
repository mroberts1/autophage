## Abstract

> The researchers investigate the consequences of training machine learning models on data generated by other AI systems. They identify a phenomenon called "Model Collapse," demonstrating that when language models and other generative models learn from AI-generated content, they develop irreversible degradation in performance, losing diversity in their outputs. The team shows this effect appears across multiple model types including variational autoencoders and gaussian mixture models. Their key finding suggests that "tails of the original content distribution disappear," potentially threatening model quality as AI-generated content becomes increasingly prevalent online.

## Notes

One of the most widely cited early papers on model collapse. Coined or popularized the term "Model Collapse." A revised and extended version was published in Nature 631(8022):755-759, July 25, 2024: "AI Models Collapse when Trained on Recursively Generated Data." The key image of "tails of the distribution disappearing" became influential framing.
