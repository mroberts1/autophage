## Summary

A retrospective institutional profile piece from Rice University's alumni magazine, Winter 2025. Covers the full arc of the Baraniuk group's MAD research program with Baraniuk's own voice.

Key points:
- Traces the origin of the MAD research at Rice DSP Lab
- Baraniuk explains the basic mechanism: "after even a few generations of such training, the new models can become irreparably corrupted"
- Synthesizes the data scarcity argument: as real data is exhausted, synthetic data is tempting but dangerous
- Notes that MAD affects both image models (visual artifacts) and LLMs
- Escalation warning: "if left uncontrolled for many generations, MAD could poison the data quality and diversity of the entire internet"

This is the most authoritative popular account of the research from the group that named MAD, with direct quotes from Baraniuk.
