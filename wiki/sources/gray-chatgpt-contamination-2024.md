## Abstract

> This study employs keywords disproportionately found in LLM-generated text to estimate how prevalently such tools assisted in creating scholarly literature. Analysis of 2023 publications reveals several keywords appeared unusually frequently both individually and in combination. The research concludes "at least 60,000 papers (slightly over 1% of all articles) were LLM-assisted," though further refinement through additional analysis remains possible.

## Notes

Empirical estimate of LLM contamination in academic publishing using stylometric fingerprinting (detecting over-represented LLM-typical phrases). The 1%+ figure for 2023 is likely an undercount and early signal of a growing trend. Directly relevant to the self-consuming loop: if scholarly literature trains future LLMs, LLM-written scholarship creates a feedback loop.
