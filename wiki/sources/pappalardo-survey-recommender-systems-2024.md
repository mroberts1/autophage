## Abstract

> The paper presents a systematic review examining how recommendation systems affect users, items, and human-AI interactions across four distinct contexts: social media, online retail, urban mapping, and generative AI. The authors analyzed 154 articles to create a taxonomy of research methodologies (empirical, simulation, observational, controlled) and documented outcomes including "concentration, content degradation, discrimination, diversity, echo chamber, filter bubble, homogenisation, polarisation, radicalisation, volume." The work aims to unify fragmented terminology across disciplines and provide insights for scholars, policymakers, and technology companies.
