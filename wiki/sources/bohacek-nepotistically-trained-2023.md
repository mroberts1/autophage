## Abstract

> When generative-AI image models are retrained on their own outputs, "these generative-AI models produce highly distorted images." The research shows this degradation spreads beyond the specific prompts used in retraining and that recovery remains incomplete even after subsequent training on authentic images.

## Notes

The term "nepotistically trained" is the authors' coinage for models retrained on their own outputs. Notably documents that collapse is not easily reversed by subsequent training on real data. Image generation domain.
